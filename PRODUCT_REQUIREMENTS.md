# Noir House product requirements

## Future named hotel assistant

- Introduce the assistant only after the communications platform has accumulated enough clean, reliable operational history for useful pattern analysis.
- Give it a warm, trusted hospitality name (working examples: **Jeeves** or **Dave**) and a restrained visual identity.
- Present it honestly as a digital or AI-assisted hotel helper; never imply that it is a human member of staff.
- Use gentle, non-blocking nudges rather than intrusive pop-ups. Suggestions must include the evidence or pattern behind them.
- Useful patterns include repeated equipment faults, recurring room-preparation delays, repeated guest issues, stock shortages, slow responses, reopened tasks, and urgency misuse.
- Keep important decisions human-approved. The assistant may recommend, summarize, and draw attention; it must not silently authorize spending, change guest arrangements, discipline staff, or close operational incidents.
- Log each suggestion, who viewed it, whether it was accepted or dismissed, and the eventual outcome so recommendations can be audited and improved.
- Provide clear controls to mute, postpone, or explain a suggestion.
