# What Helm needs to be good for a seed-stage company

A seed company moves through the nine stages, from set-up to scale. At every stage the founder needs the same seven things in one place: direction, execution, people, money, fundraising, traction and the paperwork that keeps the company clean. This list says what Helm needs for each, and what the front end already has.

Status: **Have** = built in the front end · **Partly** = started, needs more · **Missing** = not built yet

## 1. Direction: where the company is going

| Need | Why a seed founder needs it | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Company priorities with progress | The 3–5 things that matter this quarter, visible to everyone | Have |
| Stage roadmap (nine stages) | Shows where the company is and who is involved | Have |
| Initiatives | Big goals (e.g. "Launch Murmur", "Close seed") that group projects, with health and target dates | Have |
| Decisions waiting on the founder | Stops the team waiting on the CEO | Have |
| Decision log | A record of what was decided, when and why, for the team and the board | Have |

## 2. Execution: getting the work done

| Need | Why | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Projects roadmap by quarter | See all projects and add new ones | Have |
| Tasks with owner, priority, due date | Assign work to coworkers | Have |
| Board (drag between stages) | See flow and blockers | Have |
| Blockers: who is waiting on whom | The biggest cause of slow teams at seed | Have |
| My tasks view | Each person sees only their own work | Have |
| Team check-ins / weekly updates | Each team posts a short update so the founder doesn't chase people | Partly (team pulse) |
| Notes and meeting notes | 1:1s, board prep, investor calls, decisions in writing | Have |

## 3. People and hiring

| Need | Why | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Team directory with roles | Who does what, who covers which role | Partly (lean team table) |
| Hiring pipeline | Candidates by stage (applied → interview → offer → hired) for each open role | Missing |
| Offers and start dates | Know when people join and what was offered | Missing |
| Onboarding checklist | New hires get set up the same way every time | Missing |
| Contractors and IP assignment | Missing IP assignments from early contractors is a common diligence problem | Missing |

## 4. Money

| Need | Why | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Runway and monthly burn | The number every seed founder watches | Have |
| Spend vs plan, by category | Spot overspending early | Have |
| Cash in the bank | The real balance, not just burn | Missing |
| Revenue / MRR | Separate recurring from one-off revenue | Missing |
| Budget by team | Each team's spend against its budget | Missing |
| Upcoming bills and subscriptions | No surprise payments | Missing |

## 5. Fundraising

| Need | Why | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rounds and amount raised | Pre-seed, seed, target vs committed | Have |
| Investor pipeline (CRM) | Every investor by stage, ticket, owner, next step and notes | Partly (table only) |
| Cap table summary | Who owns what, SAFEs and option pool; investors ask for a clean cap table | Missing |
| SAFE / convertible tracker | Caps, discounts and amounts on every note | Missing |
| Data room checklist | What's ready for diligence: deck, model, cap table, contracts, IP, P&L, KPIs | Missing |
| Investor update writer and history | Monthly updates keep investors warm for the next round | Missing (button only) |

## 6. Customers and traction

| Need | Why | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| KPI dashboard with targets | Users, retention, MRR, growth rate; investors expect growing metrics before you raise | Partly (waitlist, runway) |
| Pilot / customer pipeline | Track pilots from first call to paying | Partly (priority only) |
| Customer feedback | Collect what users say and link it to tasks | Missing |

## 7. Board, legal and documents

| Need | Why | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Files and media | Decks, videos, designs, contracts in one place | Have |
| Board meetings | Agenda, board pack, minutes, approvals | Missing |
| Compliance calendar | Filing deadlines, tax dates, 83(b) elections, renewals | Missing |
| Contracts library | Customer, vendor and employment contracts with renewal dates | Missing |

## 8. Communication

| Need | Why | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Messages (spaces and direct) | Team talks in one place, with @mentions and task links | Have |
| Write a message from anywhere | The "Message" drawer | Have |
| Search everything | Tasks, projects, files, people, messages | Have |
| Company announcements | One-way updates from the founder to everyone | Missing |
| Notifications | What changed that involves me | Missing |

## 9. Setting up and running the workspace

| Need | Why | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First-run set-up | Create the company, pick the current stage, invite the team | Missing |
| Members, roles and permissions | Investors and contractors should not see everything | Missing |
| Settings | Company details, currency, financial year | Missing |

## Front-end build plan

Backend comes later. Each batch is front end only, with sample data.

**Batch 1: founder must-haves**
1. Notes (meeting notes, 1:1s, board prep) with a list and an editor
2. Initiatives (like Linear's view: goals, health, projects done, active projects)
3. Fundraising hub: investor pipeline board, cap table summary, SAFE tracker, data room checklist, investor update writer
4. People and hiring: team directory and hiring pipeline board

**Batch 2: numbers and governance**
5. Metrics dashboard: MRR, users, retention, growth rate, each with a target
6. Money upgrades: cash balance, budget by team, upcoming bills
7. Board and legal: board meetings with minutes, compliance calendar, contracts with renewal dates
8. Decision log

**Batch 3: workspace**
9. First-run set-up (company, stage, invite team)
10. Members, roles and permissions; settings
11. Notifications and company announcements
12. Customer feedback linked to tasks
